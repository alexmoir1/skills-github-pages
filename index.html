About

Grace works across GCSE and A Level Art, alongside A Level Graphic Communication, moving fluidly between observational drawing, acrylic painting, and digital illustration.

Recurring interests run through the work: the human figure in motion, character and narrative illustration, and design for print — from book covers to campaign posters and a fully illustrated children's book.
